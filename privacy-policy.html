Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Information you choose to share

This portfolio does not have a server-side contact form or database. Submitting the contact form opens a draft in your own email application; nothing is sent by this website until you choose to send that email. Any information in the message is then handled by your email provider and the recipient.

Preferences stored in your browser

The site saves your selected color theme and font preference in your browser’s local storage so those display settings can be remembered on your next visit. These preferences stay in your browser and are not submitted to this portfolio.

Third-party services and hosting

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google to retrieve those font files, subject to Google’s own privacy terms. The site is hosted as a static website; the hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address and browser information as part of operating and securing the service.

Links to other websites

This portfolio links to external services such as LinkedIn, GitHub, and certificate providers. Those websites have their own privacy practices, which apply when you visit them.
