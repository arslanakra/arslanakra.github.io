CRITICAL BUGS CAUGHT

BUG-001 CRITICAL

Fund Field Mapping & Sign Inversion

Check Cashing Web App - Money Transfer

Found that the API returned the additional service transaction amount under the maxi parameter, while the frontend rendered it in the intermex field, posting transactions to the wrong service category on daily financial sheets. Also found that negative amounts returned by the API were being converted to positive values by the React component rendering the transaction, inverting the sign and mismatching totals against money transfer debits.

Impact: Financial reporting and debit reconciliation errors across daily transaction sheets.

Status: FIXED

BUG-002 CRITICAL

OCR Failure Payload & Terminal Session Scoping

Agent POS System

Found that when Gemini OCR failed to parse a check, the system still forwarded routing number, account number, check number, and owner information to the maker instead of blocking submission on an empty or invalid payload, risking downstream processing of unverified checks. Separately found that users provisioned for Terminal 890 could authenticate and transact on Terminal 891, breaking terminal-level access control.

Impact: Business continuity risk from unverified check processing, plus broken terminal-level access control.

Status: FIXED

BUG-003 CRITICAL

Stale Navigation State After Wallet and Prepaid Detachment

MySalarium - Send Money App

After detaching the wallet and prepaid sections from the app, the newer build was scoped to open into a calculator screen first, letting users calculate the real-time send and receive amount across countries before proceeding. In the detachment APK and TestFlight build, found that backgrounding the app while the calculator screen was open, then killing and relaunching it, caused the app to route back to the old wallet section instead of resuming on the calculator. Also found that after completing sign-in and registration, the app navigated to the prepaid section instead of the recipients list, which was the intended post-registration flow in the new build.

Impact: Users landed on deprecated screens (wallet, prepaid) that were supposed to be fully detached from the app, breaking the calculator-first flow on relaunch and the correct post-registration flow into the recipients list.

Status: FIXED
